# Reference analysis and production prompt

## Reference observations
- Elevated three-quarter view, complete board visible, long-lens product photography.
- Thick charcoal frame on a warm finely textured beige plaster surface.
- 8 by 8 glossy enamel tiles alternate sapphire star fields and ivory cumulus clouds. Peach, lavender and golden sunset at the center.
- Exactly 32 traditional chess pieces: polished blue-black obsidian at the far side, translucent ivory and amber glass at the near side.
- Light concentrated in the circular bases, creamy broad highlights, tiny blue and gold sparkles.
- Broad warm key, cool fill, studio reflections, directional shadows and soft warm pools on the floor.

## Implementation prompt
Build a full-window orbitable 3D product diorama matching the reference. Use an 8.42-unit charcoal frame, individually inset enamel tiles and 32 sculpted pieces at their starting positions. Model smooth turned bodies, beveled horse silhouettes, crenellated rooks, pointed bishops, queen crowns and king crosses. Keep the board as the sole subject. Use physical glass/obsidian materials, HDR studio reflections with large softbox cards, warm directional light, restrained cool rim light, local amber/cyan bounce, contact occlusion and subtle highlight bloom. Preserve navy saturation and photographic beige shadows. Default to an elevated three-quarter camera with all corners visible. Preserve orbit, pan and zoom; add touch zoom and keyboard reset. Fit the full scene on narrow displays. Inspect desktop and mobile renders against the reference. Real-time rasterization approximates offline path-traced glass caustics.
