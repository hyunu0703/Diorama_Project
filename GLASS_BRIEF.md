# Glass rendering acceptance brief

Keep the empty PC chassis and blurred gallery background. Use untinted, optically clear glass with sharp transmission, 1.52 index of refraction and 3 mm optical thickness. Use a captured high dynamic range studio environment, with visible white softbox reflections that move across the glass when orbiting. Do not simulate clarity by reducing global material opacity, and do not cover the glass in a gray diffuse layer. Preserve empty interior, metal back panels and chassis dimensions. Verify the published material from multiple camera angles. Do not describe the result as photorealistic based on parameter values alone.

Lighting asset: Poly Haven, Studio Small 09, 1K HDR, CC0.
https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09
